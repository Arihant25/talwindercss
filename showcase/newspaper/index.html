Lead story · By our Chief Correspondent

Monsoon returns after week-long lull, fishermen advised to stay ashore

Coastal districts brace for heavy showers till Friday morning. Suburban rail services expected to run late.

The Regional Meteorological Centre upgraded its forecast on Tuesday evening, saying a low-pressure area off the Bay of Bengal had strengthened faster than expected. Rainfall of upto 12 centimetres is now likely across Kanyakumari, Ramanathapuram, and Nagapattinam districts by Thursday night.

Fishermen have been asked to return to port immediately. Sixteen mechanised boats were still at sea as of Tuesday evening, according to figures released by the Fisheries Department. Local associations at Kasimedu and Marina harbours have set up radio contact for the crews.

The city corporation opened four temporary shelters in low-lying wards along the Cooum. Storm-water drains near Anna Salai are being tested for capacity, though officials admitted that the network south of Nandanam is still under construction.

Suburban rail services from Beach to Tambaram are expected to run 10 to 15 minutes late from Wednesday afternoon. School holiday announcement is being considered by the district collector.

National

Cabinet clears fresh subsidy for cotton growers

The Union Cabinet on Tuesday approved a Rs. 1,700 crore support package for cotton growers in five states. The scheme covers three quintals per acre and will be paid directly into bank accounts linked to the farmer's Aadhaar.

Farmer bodies in Maharashtra and Telangana welcomed the move but said the ceiling should be raised to five quintals. The Minister of Agriculture is expected to visit Nagpur next week.

Editorial · A fair rain

Every October we are told the drainage will hold. Every November it does not. This is not a failure of the monsoon. It is a failure of the maintenance schedule. The corporation has the equipment. What it lacks is a reason to act between rains.

A modest suggestion: publish the desilting log online, ward by ward. Let residents know when the drain outside their gate was last cleared. Public records have a way of getting the work done.

"The umbrella budget was sanctioned in 2019. We're still procuring the umbrellas."
— R. K. Ramesh, Cartoonist
